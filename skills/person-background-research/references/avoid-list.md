# What to flag

Check the dossier against each category. For every hit, add a line to the avoid list with what it is, why it's risky, and a source. Flag, don't hide: the user needs to know so they don't step on it by accident.

## Stay away from
- **Recent professional pain:** layoffs they ran or were caught in, firing or forced exit, company shutdown, down round, failed launch, missed earnings, lawsuits, regulatory trouble.
- **Public controversy:** criticism, pile-ons, investigations, retracted work, anything they've publicly apologized for.
- **Politics and religion:** political donations, campaign work, strong political posts, religious affiliation — even if public.
- **Personal hardship:** illness, death in the family, divorce, mental health — even if they've written about it movingly. Don't reference it in a cold email.
- **Job change in flight:** just left, just announced leaving, or rumored to be leaving.
- **Family specifics:** anything beyond the self-disclosed basics. Never mention kids by name.

## Background only (👀)
These help the user understand the person but would feel like surveillance in a cold email:
- Where they live, beyond what's in their public bio
- Vacations and travel spots
- Family details
- Anything from personal posts more than a couple of years old
- Anything that took more than a few clicks to find

## Overused hooks
Note what the person is most famous for or most often asked about. Mentioning it isn't wrong, but leading with it signals the user didn't dig deeper.
